Campus Event · Release 1

02

Before launch

The college plans to launch Campus Event, a web application for discovering and booking campus events.

Situation

Orientation week begins on Monday. Missing the launch would create extra manual work for organisers and reduce the time students have to discover events.

The team reports that it delivered the requested features on Friday and that the planned demonstration succeeded.

Sponsor request

Students need one place to discover events, reserve a place, and receive a reminder. Please launch before orientation week.

Demonstrated features

  • browse upcoming events;
  • filter and view event details;
  • see remaining places;
  • reserve or cancel one place;
  • receive a confirmation;
  • allow organisers to view reservations.

Print “Release 1 evidence packet” for the pre-break packet. Keep Release 2 unavailable until the learning gate.

03

What the team knew

Demonstration record

Friday · test environment

  1. A team member opened an event showing 18 remaining places.
  2. They selected Reserve once.
  3. A confirmation page appeared.
  4. The count changed to 17.
  5. The organiser view showed the reservation.
  6. The confirmation email arrived.

Result recorded by the team: Passed.

Two definitions of success

Sponsor

The essential outcome is launching before orientation week with the agreed features.

Event organiser

I need the reservation list and remaining-place count to be correct. Some rooms have strict capacity limits.

Release decision

After the demonstration, the team approved the Monday launch and wrote:

Everything requested is complete, the demonstration passed, and the launch date is protected.

Your task before Release 2

Individually record what the evidence justifies concluding about launch readiness and one important uncertainty it does not settle. Then agree as a team on one packet fact supporting readiness and one assumption or uncertainty.

Do not invent events outside the packet. Label unsupported ideas as hypotheses or questions.

After launch

Campus Event opens on Monday morning. Reservations begin for a popular welcome event with 40 places.

Launch-day evidence · 09:05–09:07

Support receives a report that Reserve appeared to do nothing. Two minutes later, the organiser sees 41 names for the 40-place event, including two reservations for one student.

Support ticket 184

I pressed Reserve, waited, and saw no confirmation. I pressed it again and then received two confirmation emails. My account shows two reservations for the same event.

Support ticket 191

The event said one place remained. My friend and I both tried to reserve it. We both saw a success message, but the organiser says only one of us can attend.

Print this packet separately. Distribute it only after the Release 1 learning gate.

Campus Event · Release 2

05

What changed?

Requirement note

Show students whether places are available and confirm a reservation when booking succeeds.

The note does not define what should happen when two requests compete for the last place, when a response is delayed, or when a student retries after seeing no confirmation.

The evidence shows unreliable booking outcomes. It does not establish the implementation mechanism that caused them.

Your revised task

Do not erase or rewrite your Release 1 claim.

  1. Record the new evidence that changes or qualifies it.
  2. Write your revised claim.
  3. Identify one important thing that remains unknown.

Then complete one team causal strip on the evidence revision sheet. Several explanations may be defensible; distinguish packet evidence from inference.
