Session 11 · Maintenance and portfolio handover

Can another team safely continue your work?

First, an owned maintenance response. Then, feedback and revision on your portfolio.

Formative engineering handover

Reader asks. Author listens. Evidence decides.

Author

Identify the passage. Let the reader infer the decision.

Reader

Reconstruct the decision, reason, and uncertainty before feedback.

Author

Record feedback without defending every sentence.

Both

Choose one revision that remains the author’s work.

Review note

Peer feedback should identify reasoning opportunities. The author decides and writes the revision.

Anchor · if you lose the thread

The reader reconstructs before the author explains.

Say · rehearsal script

The author identifies one bounded passage and stays quiet while the reader reads. The reader reconstructs the decision, its reason, and the remaining uncertainty. Only then should you discuss a consequential gap. Use the added rehearsal time to try one neutral reconstruction question and one overreaching rewrite, then name the difference.

Ask, “What evidence supports this choice?” Do not supply the choice or rewrite the passage. The author records the feedback and later decides what to change. [Explain the private alternative: reconstruct the example, then self-review the own draft. It cannot show another reader understood that draft.]

Rescue or cut

Explanation5 min

Not every comment deserves adoption

Use feedback as evidence, not instruction

Adopt

It exposes a consequential gap.

Adapt

The issue is real but the suggested response is too large.

Reject

It conflicts with evidence, scope, or the accepted brief.

Ask

The comment reveals a question you cannot yet resolve.

Review note

A justified rejection can show sound engineering judgement. Record why.
