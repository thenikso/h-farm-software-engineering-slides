From wishes to testable requirements

Can a requirement sound precise and still be useless?

Today, another team must be able to act and check.

Textbook study

§4.4, pp. 120-124, and §4.5, pp. 129-130. Printed pages in Sommerville (2016), 10th edition, Global Edition. See the session 4 reading guide for the study question and optional selections.

Session question

Specific-looking wording can still hide an unclear behaviour, unmeasurable quality, unsupported choice, or missing uncertainty.

Explanation7 min

Four tests

BehaviourWhat must happen?
ConditionWhen or for whom?
EvidenceHow could we check?
BoundaryWhat remains open?

Useful, not exhaustive

A requirement should support shared understanding and a later check. It may still carry an explicit uncertainty.

Anchor · if you lose the thread

Check behaviour, condition, evidence, and boundary.

Say · rehearsal script

What must happen, under what condition, and what would someone observe? Then ask what the wording leaves open. Those four questions help us inspect usefulness. Check behaviour, condition, evidence, and boundary.

A pending decision does not make every sentence unusable. We can state what is supported and name the assumption that still needs agreement. The important thing is not to disguise the assumption as a fact. Keep the reasoning inspectable. [Pause for thinking, responses, or the stated work time before moving on.]

Delivery guidance · not spoken

Listen for

Transition

Separate system behaviour from system quality.

Explanation8 min

Functional

What the system must do

Meaningful behaviour, service, or response.

Non-functional

How well or under what limits

A quality, constraint, or operating condition.

Keep the relationship

Quality requirements shape behaviours. “Fast” is not useful until the relevant operation, measure, threshold, and conditions are visible.

Anchor · if you lose the thread

Connect the behaviour to its quality.

Say · rehearsal script

A functional requirement says what service or response the system provides. A non-functional requirement concerns a quality or constraint on that behaviour. Booking a place and returning a usable response are related, but they answer different questions. Connect the behaviour to its quality.

Use the retry and keyboard statements on the sheet. Which describes a response and which limits how that response must be delivered? Some wording contains both; separate the claims when that makes them easier to check. Keep the reasoning inspectable. [Pause for thinking, responses, or the stated work time before moving on.]

Delivery guidance · not spoken

Example aloud

Booking a place is behaviour. Response time during a known load is a measurable quality of that behaviour.

Boundary

Do not turn this into a catalogue of quality attributes.

Explanation8 min

Replace the adjective

QualityWhat matters?
MeasureWhat is observed?
ThresholdWhat is acceptable?
ConditionUnder what load or context?

Measurability

The measure must fit the stakeholder outcome. A conveniently measurable number can still be irrelevant.

Anchor · if you lose the thread

A number needs a reason and conditions.

Say · rehearsal script

For a quality, name what matters, what we measure, an acceptable result, and the conditions of measurement. Two seconds for which action, on what connection, with how many people using it? A number needs a reason and conditions.

Our Campus Event sheet gives no agreed performance target. You can propose one with a reason and an approval question, or preserve the missing decision. Inventing a precise number does not create stakeholder agreement. Keep the reasoning inspectable. [Pause for thinking, responses, or the stated work time before moving on.]

Delivery guidance · not spoken

Say

“Do not invent a threshold because a blank asks for one. State what evidence or stakeholder decision would justify it.”

Listen for

Percentages without a population, time window, environment, or source.

Explanation6 min

Acceptance criteria

Name the requirement, then make pass or fail decidable

Given / When / Then is permitted. Observable conditions matter more than the syntax.

Acceptance boundary

Criteria check a named functional requirement. They do not need to describe every test or implementation detail.

Anchor · if you lose the thread

Make the acceptance result observable.

Say · rehearsal script

Choose a named requirement and describe a setup, action, and result that would pass or fail. Given, When, Then is one way to organise those claims, but the words themselves do not make a criterion useful. Make the acceptance result observable.

For example, if a requirement says cancellation releases a place, a check must inspect both the cancelled reservation and availability. We should also identify any unresolved rule that stops us deciding the expected result. Keep the reasoning inspectable. [Pause for thinking, responses, or the stated work time before moving on.]

Delivery guidance · not spoken

Contrast aloud

“Works correctly” repeats the claim. A criterion identifies setup, action, and observable result.

Comparison focus
