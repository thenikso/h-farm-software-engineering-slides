Post-assessment boundary

The reflection is private and non-graded; any optional collection uses the announced anonymous route. It asks where decisions consumed time, not what answer was correct.

Say · rehearsal script

The next activity is ungraded. We will reflect on the process without comparing answers. Use the reflection page to consider where your attention went, and leave out scenario details. [Allow a quiet reset.]

Delivery guidance · not spoken

Say

Contingency

Say · rehearsal script

Think back to one point where making a decision took longer than you expected. Name the kind of decision and what made it difficult, without reproducing your assessment response. You can write privately; you do not have to discuss it. [Give quiet writing time.]

Then identify one habit you want to retain or change. Make it something you can do next time, such as leaving time to reread the brief. If you contribute to the shared reflection, share only a process label, not your answer.

Your turn20 min · on your own

Choose a tentative route and write one question that must be answered before you invest further.

Seed, not mini-portfolio

The card helps choose a workable context. It is not assessed and should remain small enough to change.

Say · rehearsal script

Use the seed card to make your tentative choice concrete. Write a bounded decision and the evidence it would need. Include the situation, people affected, intended outcome, and one exclusion. [Allow individual writing.]

Where you do not have evidence, write a question. Keep assumptions visible; do not invent research or user agreement to make the seed look finished. The card is yours to revise.

Delivery guidance · not spoken

Listen for

A topic label without a project decision, or a project too broad to support specific evidence.

Rescue
