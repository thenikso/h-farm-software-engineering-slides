Choose a relevant answer

Slide 14 · with your partner · keep Part A beside you

These are fictional answers for this exercise, not diagnoses of booking faults.

  1. Start with the stakeholder you asked in box 3. Choose one answer whose words address the decision your question tests. The same role is not enough.
  2. Write its ID and stakeholder in box 5. Keep this page attached; you do not need to copy the answer.
  3. No answer fits? In box 5, write “Not answered”, who you asked and what is missing. Keep box 2 unchanged; in box 6, explain what stays provisional pending evidence.

A1 · Sponsor

“I want fewer places left unused because students are unsure whether they have a reservation. I would accept a smaller first change if it gives us evidence about that problem. I have not agreed a numerical attendance target.”

A2 · Student

“After pressing Reserve I need to know whether I have a place. When the connection is slow, an email arriving later does not tell me what to do now. I would look for a clear status before pressing again.”

A3 · Organiser

“Count a student as confirmed only when their reservation is recorded. Saying they will come, or pressing Reserve without seeing a result, does not give them a place, and a confirmed place still does not tell me who actually attended. I set each event's capacity and cancellation rules, but I cannot confirm from these reports how the software currently handles competing requests.”

A4 · Support

“I need a reliable way to look up a student's reservation for the named event and explain its current status. An email screenshot alone is insufficient. We have not agreed who may access that lookup or what details they need.”

A5 · Accessibility support

“A student must be able to operate the reservation journey by keyboard and perceive the result with a screen reader. We can review that journey with representative users, but this has not happened yet. One successful check would not establish accessibility for everyone.”

A6 · Delivery lead

“This week we can investigate one unclear journey with the organiser and student volunteers. We do not yet know the repair effort. If the sponsor wants reminders first, we need to explain what booking uncertainty remains and ask for a decision.”

You may read all six to check relevance, but they are not all answers to your question. Start with one answer. To use a second, write a follow-up question and why it matters under box 7, then record both IDs.
