Whose answer would change the frame?

Campus Event is a browser-based service for discovering events, reserving or cancelling places, and receiving useful reminders. Organisers need trustworthy capacity and attendance information. This sheet adds fictional stakeholder testimony for today’s elicitation exercise; it does not explain the cause of earlier booking faults.

Six statements · unresolved perspectives

Sponsor “We need a reminder feature for the next event. Empty seats make the launch look unsuccessful.”

Student “When my phone gives me no clear response, I do not know if I have a place. I may try again.”

Organiser “I need to know who has a confirmed place before I send event information. An interested student is not the same as an attendee.”

Support “Students ask us whether they are booked. We should not have to guess from an email screenshot.”

Accessibility support “The reservation outcome must be available to someone using a keyboard and screen reader, not just a colour change.”

Delivery lead “We can investigate one unclear journey with organisers and students this week. We cannot promise every suggested feature for the next event.”

Read every perspective. Mark one shared outcome, one conflict, and one assumption. No stakeholder priority hierarchy has been agreed.
